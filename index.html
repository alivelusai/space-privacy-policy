Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Thank you for using Space. This Privacy Policy explains what information Space collects, how it is used and stored, who processes it, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

Information We Collect

Account information

You sign in with your Google account through Firebase Authentication. We receive a Firebase user ID and an ID token for each sign-in. Firebase Authentication also holds the basic profile details Google provides for your account, such as your name and email address. We use these to identify your account and apply your plan limits.

Device identifier

The app creates a random identifier for each installation and sends it with requests. We use it to apply free usage limits, to link attachments to the device that uploaded them, and to track image requests per device. It is not your name, phone number, or advertising ID.

Chat messages

When you send a message, the text, the earlier messages in the conversation, and any files attached to the conversation are sent to our servers and then to an AI model provider. Free chats are answered by GenzDealz. Space Pro chats are answered by Anthropic. Your messages are not used to train models by us.

Attachments

If you attach a photo, PDF, text, Markdown, CSV, or other supported file, it is uploaded to our servers. Original attachments are stored in private cloud storage under your account so they can be used in your conversations and retrieved on your other signed-in devices. Supported types are PDF, plain text, Markdown, CSV, JPEG, PNG, GIF, and WebP, up to 50 MB per file.

Chat and creation backups

When you are signed in, your chat history and creations are backed up to private cloud storage so they can be restored when you sign in on another device. Deleted conversations are recorded as deleted so they are not restored from an older copy.

Image requests

When you ask Space to create or edit an image, your prompt and any source images you choose are sent to our image generation provider, fal.ai, which returns the generated image. We keep a reference to the most recent image for each conversation for 30 days so that follow-up edits work.

Web search and link reading

When a question needs current information, or when you include a web address, the search terms or address are sent to public sources (Google News and Wikipedia) and to Jina AI, a web reading service, which fetches the page text. These services receive the search terms or web address and the network information of our servers. They do not receive your Google account details.

Purchase information

If you subscribe to Space Pro or buy a token top-up, Google Play processes the payment. We receive the purchase token and the product and subscription details from Google Play so we can verify the purchase, and we link it to your account. We do not receive your payment card details.

Usage counts

We record how many chats, image generations, and AI tokens you use, so that plan limits can be enforced and top-up credits are tracked.

Technical and security data

Our servers handle requests from your device and may log errors and the connecting network address. We use this to keep the service running and to limit abuse.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your chat history, settings, and creations in the app's private storage on your device. This data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. The app does not use Android automatic backup, so this data is not copied to your Google Drive by Android. Chats and creations are also backed up to your account as described above when you are signed in.

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • sign you in and keep your account and plan up to date;
  • answer your messages, create and edit images, and run the web searches you request;
  • enforce free and Space Pro usage limits, and verify and credit Google Play purchases;
  • restore your chats and creations when you sign in on another device;
  • keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising.

Permissions

  • Internet: to connect to our servers.
  • Photos, videos, and files: you choose them through your device's system picker. The app reads only the files you select. Space does not request access to your full photo library or storage.

Your Choices

You can delete individual chats and creations in the app at any time. You can delete your Space account and all associated data from within the app (see Account Deletion below). You can also stop using the app at any time and uninstall it to remove the data stored on your device.

Account Deletion

Deleting your account in the app. Open Settings, tap Delete account, and confirm. This permanently deletes:

  • your chat history, creations, and attachments stored on our servers;
  • your usage counters and token balances;
  • your Space sign-in account in Firebase Authentication.

Chats and creations stored on your device are also removed from the app. Deletion cannot be undone.

Space Pro subscriptions. Cancel your Space Pro subscription in Google Play before you delete your account. If you do not, Google may continue to charge you after your account is deleted. Any paid time left on your subscription is lost when your account is deleted.

Purchase records. After deletion we keep a record of each Google Play purchase that was used on Space. The record is not linked to your name or to your deleted account. It prevents the same purchase from being credited twice or used to claim Space Pro on another account.

If you cannot access the app. Email alivelusainikhil786@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Google account, with the subject "Delete my Space account". We will verify the request and delete your data within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Space is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Requests between the app and our servers are sent over HTTPS. Access to your account data requires a valid Firebase sign-in token. Purchases are verified with Google Play on our servers. Storage is private and not publicly accessible. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
