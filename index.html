Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Thank you for using Space. This Privacy Policy explains what information Space collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Account information. You can sign in with Google. We receive your Google account identifier (Firebase user ID) and an ID token from Firebase Authentication. We use these to identify your account and apply your plan limits.

Device identifier. The app creates a random identifier for your installation and sends it with requests to our servers. It is used to apply free usage limits and to link image and chat requests to your installation. It is not linked to your name or phone number.

Chat messages and attachments. When you send a message, the text is sent to our servers and then to the AI model provider. If you attach a photo, video, or file, it is uploaded to our servers so that it can be used in your conversation. Messages, attachments, and generated images you save to your account may be stored in cloud storage so they can be restored on your other devices.

Image requests. Prompts, and any source images you choose to edit, are sent to our image generation provider to create the result.

Web search requests. When you ask a question that needs current information, your search query, and any URL you provide, are sent to public web sources and a web reading service to fetch results.

Purchase information. If you subscribe to Space Pro or buy a token top-up, Google Play processes the payment. We receive a purchase token and the subscription or purchase details from Google Play to verify your purchase. We do not receive your payment card details.

Usage counts. We record the number of chat requests, image generations, and AI tokens you use, so that plan limits can be enforced.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your chat history, settings, and creations in the app's private storage on your device. This data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Chat history and creations may also be backed up to your account as described above.

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • sign you in and keep your account and plan up to date;
  • answer your messages, generate images, and perform web searches you request;
  • enforce free and Pro usage limits and verify purchases;
  • restore your chats and creations when you sign in on another device;
  • keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising.

Third-Party Services

Space relies on these providers to operate:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing);
  • AI model providers that process your chat messages and attachments;
  • An image generation provider that processes your image prompts and source images;
  • Cloud object storage for attachments and account backups;
  • Public web sources and a web reading service for search and link requests;
  • Upstash Redis for usage counters and purchase records.

Each provider processes data only to provide its part of the service. Their own privacy policies apply to the data they process.

Data Retention

  • Usage counters are kept only as long as needed to enforce limits. Pending usage holds expire after 15 minutes, and usage records are kept for up to 45 days.
  • Account backups and attachments are kept until you delete them or delete your account.

Permissions

  • Internet: to connect to our servers.
  • Photos, videos, and files: selected through your device's system pickers. The app only reads the files you choose.

Children's Privacy

Space is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Requests are sent over HTTPS. Access to your account data is protected by Firebase Authentication, and purchases are verified with Google Play on our servers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You can delete chats and creations in the app. You can request deletion of your account data by contacting us. You can stop using the app at any time and uninstall it to remove local data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
